{% else %} {# Before calculating: a short explanation of the formulas #}

How it works

1. BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor equation)

Men: 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5 Women: 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age − 161

2. TDEE = BMR × activity multiplier (1.2 – 1.9)

3. Goal calories = TDEE adjusted for your goal

BMI is a screening measurement, not a diagnosis. It only uses height and weight, so it can't tell muscle from fat. A muscular person can be "Overweight" by BMI while having low body fat, and BMI says nothing about where fat is stored.
