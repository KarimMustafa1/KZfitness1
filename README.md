# KZfitness

KZfitness is a personal computer science project inspired by my interest in fitness, strength training, and helping people improve themselves.

I wanted to explore how programming and AI tools could make tracking macros and logging lifts easier for beginners and people like me who go to the gym.

With KZfitness, you don't have to keep going back and forth with an AI chatbot just to ask how many calories are in a chicken breast. The app includes an AI fitness assistant designed to help answer questions about nutrition, including protein, carbohydrates, and calories, as well as provide advice on workout splits tailored to your fitness information.

By entering details such as your age, sex, weight, and height, you can get more personalized guidance, to make macro tracking simpler and more convenient.

## Features

Macro Tracking: Track daily calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats to stay on top of your nutrition goals.

Workout Logging: Record your lifts and exercises to keep track of your gym sessions and progress.

AI Fitness Assistant: Chat with an embedded AI assistant for nutrition information, calorie estimates, and fitness advice.

Personalized Guidance: Get fitness and nutrition recommendations based on your personal information and goals.

Fitness Calculators: Calculate BMI, estimate daily calorie needs, and explore other fitness-related measurements like the special one rep max calculator!

Progress Tracking: Review your nutrition and workout data to better understand your progress over time over a graph.

## My goal

I built KZfitness to explore how computer science and AI can solve a problem I experience myself. I want to keep improving the application while learning more about programming, web development, and integrating AI into useful tools.
